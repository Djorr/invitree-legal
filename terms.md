*Last updated: 10 October 2026*

## 1. The service

Invitree is a Discord bot with a web dashboard that tracks invites and offers related features. The free plan is provided as is. Premium is a paid subscription per server.

- Server admins are responsible for how they use the data Invitree shows them, such as kicking members based on fake flags.

## 3. Giveaways

Giveaways run through Invitree are organised by the server, not by Invitree. The server is responsible for the prize, for delivering it and for following local laws on giveaways and contests. Invitree only draws winners at random among eligible entries.

## 4. Premium and payments

- Premium costs €4.99 per month (or €49.99 per year) per server, billed through Stripe.
- It renews automatically until you cancel. After cancelling, Premium stays active until the end of the paid period.
- Payments are not refunded for partial billing periods, except where the law requires it.
- The yearly plan is billed once per year and costs the same as 10 months (2 months free).
- When Premium ends or is not renewed, the server goes back to the free limits. Your history is not deleted: older history is only hidden from view until Premium is active again.
- Prices may change; we announce price changes at least 30 days in advance and they apply from your next billing period.

We try to keep Invitree online, but we do not guarantee it is always available or free of errors. Features and limits may change. Invite counts depend on Discord's data and can occasionally be wrong (for example when two people join at the same moment).
